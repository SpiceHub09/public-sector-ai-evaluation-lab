## Prototype Limitations

- Assurance areas are currently identified using transparent rule-based indicators rather than a trained AI classifier.
- Retrieval currently uses keyword matching rather than semantic embeddings.
- Relevant policy material may therefore be missed if the terminology in the use case differs from the terminology used in the framework.
- Retrieved evidence may include sections that are only partially relevant to the use case.
- Recommended actions are generated using predefined rule-based guidance rather than a language model.
- The prototype identifies areas requiring consideration; it does not determine whether an AI system is safe, compliant or suitable for deployment.
- Findings should be reviewed by appropriately qualified technical, governance, legal, privacy and security specialists where relevant.
