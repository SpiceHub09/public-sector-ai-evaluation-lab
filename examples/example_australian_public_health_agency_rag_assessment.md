# Public Sector AI Assurance Assessment

**Assessment method:** Semantic retrieval + evidence-grounded LLM analysis

## AI Use Case

**Agency:** Australian public health agency

**AI system:** Generative AI assistant that helps public health staff find and summarise information from communicable disease policies and operational guidance

## Initial Assessment

The evidence indicates that deploying a generative AI assistant requires establishing human oversight, ensuring data quality and governance, safeguarding sensitive information, and providing staff training. Because specific technical measures for source traceability and system security are not detailed in the provided evidence, further review in those areas is recommended.

## Assurance Findings

### 1. Human Oversight and Accountability

**Finding:** Government framework guidance emphasizes human oversight and human accountability for content produced using generative AI tools.

**Why it matters:** Generative AI outputs require human discretion and judgement to ensure alignment with public health policies, legal obligations, and ethical principles.

**Recommended action:** The agency should establish procedures to ensure public health staff exercise discretion and maintain oversight and accountability when interpreting AI-generated summaries.

### 2. Data Governance and Quality

**Finding:** The framework indicates that the quality of an AI model's output is driven by the quality of its underlying data, which should be authenticated and reliable.

**Why it matters:** Using outdated, unauthenticated, or inaccurate public health guidance data could impair the quality and accuracy of the assistant's outputs.

**Recommended action:** The agency should implement data governance practices to maintain authenticated and reliable policy datasets, and consider auditing inputs and outputs to manage data quality and mitigate bias.

### 4. Staff Capability and Capability Training

**Recommended action:** The agency should ensure staff using the AI assistant are trained to evaluate outputs critically, resolve potential biases, and explain outcomes effectively.

## Further Review

- The provided evidence does not contain specific guidance on technical source traceability or handling conflicting guidance, so further review of source attribution mechanics is appropriate.
- The evidence is insufficient regarding specific IT security controls and architecture for generative AI tools, indicating technical security review is recommended.
- Further review is appropriate to determine relevant recordkeeping policy obligations applicable to generative AI tools within the specific jurisdiction.

## Important Note

This is an exploratory AI-assisted assessment. Semantic retrieval and language-model analysis can miss, misinterpret or overstate relevant guidance. The included source evidence is provided to support human verification of generated findings. The output should not be treated as a compliance, legal or deployment decision.
